# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Core Roles

### Developers

#### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

#### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Collaborate with DevOps on deployment and CI/CD optimization

#### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

#### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed
- Collaboration with Scrum Master on impediments

---

### Product Managers

#### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

#### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics
- Partner with UX Designer on user needs and validation
- Work with Stakeholder Proxy to ensure customer voice is represented

#### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs
- Collaboration with UX Designer on requirement validation

---

### Project Managers

#### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

#### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication
- Escalate blockers and facilitate resolution with support from Scrum Master

#### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation
- Partnership with Scrum Master on team health and velocity

---

## Extended Roles

### UX Designer

#### Role Summary
UX Designers lead the user research, design, and validation process to ensure features are intuitive, usable, and aligned with customer needs. They are advocates for the user throughout the project lifecycle.

#### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather design insights
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Design and validate user interfaces for usability and accessibility
- Collaborate with Product Managers to clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- Provide design assets and specifications to Developers
- Participate in demo/review sessions and gather feedback
- Advocate for user experience in prioritization discussions

#### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce design rework and usability issues
- Ensure features align with user needs and business objectives

#### Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Partner on requirements definition, success metrics, and user validation
- **Developers**: Provide design specs, collaborate on feasibility, support during implementation
- **QA**: Align on usability and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholder Proxy**: Validate design decisions with end-user representatives
- **Scrum Master**: Participate in sprint ceremonies and design review points

#### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions during planning and execution
- Design specs and asset handoffs to development team
- Participation in backlog refinement and acceptance criteria definition
- Feedback loops with QA on design validation

---

### Scrum Master / Agile Coach

#### Role Summary
Scrum Masters (or Agile Coaches) facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and help the team adopt and optimize agile practices. They serve the team and project by enabling effective collaboration and continuous improvement.

#### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, review, and retrospective ceremonies
- Identify, track, and help resolve team impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Monitor team health, velocity, and capacity
- Foster psychological safety and a culture of continuous improvement
- Support the team in adapting to changing priorities or constraints
- Escalate impediments that require PM or Product Manager intervention

#### Goals
- Enable the team to self-organize and make decisions
- Improve team velocity and delivery consistency
- Maintain team morale and reduce burnout
- Promote a learning culture through retrospectives

#### Interactions
- **Developers**: Support on process adherence, impediment resolution, and team dynamics
- **Project Manager**: Escalate blockers and support risk management; collaborate on timeline adjustments
- **Product Manager**: Ensure backlog prioritization aligns with team capacity
- **All Team Members**: Servant-leader support across all roles

#### Typical Communication
- Daily standups (15 min) and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members on well-being and obstacles
- Escalation summaries to PM on team blockers
- Retrospective follow-ups on action items

---

### DevOps Engineer

#### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure automation, and deployment processes. They enable fast, safe, and reliable releases while supporting operational excellence.

#### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration
- Monitor system performance, availability, and security
- Support deployment processes and release management
- Identify and mitigate operational risks
- Collaborate with Developers on build optimization and deployment strategies
- Maintain release runbooks and incident playbooks

#### Goals
- Enable fast, frequent, and safe releases
- Reduce deployment lead time and improve reliability
- Maintain high system performance and availability
- Reduce manual effort and toil through automation

#### Interactions
- **Developers**: Partner on build configuration, testing strategies, and deployment feedback
- **Project Manager**: Notify of operational constraints or risks that impact timeline
- **QA**: Collaborate on test automation and release gate automation
- **Release Lead / PM**: Coordinate deployment windows and rollback procedures

#### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning discussions on deployment and infrastructure needs
- Build failure notifications and troubleshooting
- Release coordination and deployment walkthroughs
- Post-incident retrospectives on operational issues

---

### Stakeholder Proxy

#### Role Summary
Stakeholder Proxies represent the voice of end users, customers, or key business stakeholders within the project team. They provide grounding and ensure the team remains focused on customer value and business outcomes.

#### Responsibilities
- Represent end-user or customer needs and perspectives in backlog prioritization
- Provide feedback and validation on proposed solutions
- Clarify business requirements and success criteria
- Communicate project status and outcomes back to broader stakeholder groups
- Escalate customer or business concerns that impact prioritization
- Participate in demos and provide acceptance feedback
- Help ensure accessibility and inclusive design considerations

#### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver customer and business value
- Reduce scope creep and misaligned features
- Build stakeholder alignment and trust
- Act as a bridge between execution team and broader organization

#### Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Collaborate on prioritization and success metrics; validate customer feedback
- **UX Designer**: Provide end-user perspective on design decisions; participate in user testing
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Project Manager**: Communicate status and decisions to broader stakeholders
- **All Team Members**: Provide regular customer/stakeholder feedback and perspective

#### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement sessions to provide stakeholder context
- Demo participation and feedback sessions
- Weekly status updates to stakeholder groups
- Ad-hoc feedback on proposed solutions or trade-offs
